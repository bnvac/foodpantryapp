      How it works

      A small warehouse system, built for volunteers who change every Saturday.

      Nothing is overwritten

      Every can in or out is a row in an append-only ledger. On-hand is the sum. A mistake gets a reversing entry, so "who logged 400 lbs of soup?" always has an answer.

      AI drafts, people confirm

      Snap a photo of a sorted pile and Claude Haiku drafts the rows: matched items, counts, legible expiry dates only. A volunteer edits and confirms. AI never writes stock.

      Forecast on time, not people

      The pantry keeps no client records. Weekly outflow is the signal: weighted recent usage gives weeks of cover, expiring stock is subtracted, and the shortfall becomes the list above.

      Infant formula is different

      Most date labels are quality dates. Formula's is a use-by, so expired batches are blocked from going out, and they don't count toward cover.

      Production design: Django 5.2 + SQLite on Railway, roles for volunteers and coordinators, CSV export everywhere so the pantry is never locked in. Source and design doc: github.com/bnvac/foodpantryapp.
