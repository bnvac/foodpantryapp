Stock control for food pantries

Know what's on the shelf, and what runs out next.

Volunteers tap what comes in and goes out. Smart Shelf forecasts each item's run-out date from real usage, and publishes a "what we need" list to the pantry's website, so donors bring the right things.

    Ways to use it

    Built for volunteers who change every week.

    Distribution day

    A volunteer at the door taps an item each time one goes in a bag. One button logs the lot.

    Give out
    Unloading a truck

    Type or paste the delivery the way you'd say it. It drafts rows; someone checks and saves. (The full version reads a photo.)

    Receive
    Closing count

    Walk one shelf, type what's there. The difference is logged as what left, so nothing has to be tapped during a rush.

    Count
    Ordering from the food bank

    Copy the shortfall in pounds, the unit food banks order in, with an order-by date that allows for delivery.

    Forecast · Export
    Thanksgiving

    Raise the season multiplier in October and every run-out date moves forward while there's still time to ask.

    Forecast
    Infant formula

    Its date is a use-by. Expired formula can't be given out and doesn't count as stock; anything near its date is flagged first.

    Expiry rules
    • Church and community pantries
    • Campus pantries
    • School backpack programs
    • Community fridges
    • Shelters and soup kitchens
    • Disaster relief depots
    • Pet food banks

    No client records, ever: it forecasts from what leaves the shelf, not who takes it. Every change is an entry in an append-only log, so any number can be traced and any mistake undone without erasing history.
